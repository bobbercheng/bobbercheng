Less checking work

Accepted · MATH-AI 2026 workshop at NeurIPS 2026

The Verification Gap

Auditing Proof Progress and Repair on IMO 2026

Bobber Cheng · Vexorium

An AI judge can spot the error. The acceptance rule can still let it pass.

This study examines how grading rules turn model judgments into acceptance decisions. A score that allows repairable errors can admit an incomplete proof. For autonomous research, the rule that authorizes progress deserves its own audit.
